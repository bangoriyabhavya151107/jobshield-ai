WHY JOBSHIELD EXISTS

Make your next move
with more clarity.

JobShield helps students, freshers and job seekers understand possible warning signs in job offers and recruiter messages.

Career opportunities should feel exciting — not confusing.

Fake job offers can use urgency, unrealistic promises and requests for money or sensitive information. JobShield gives you a structured way to review the details you have received.

Our AI assessment highlights signals in the text you submit. It does not independently investigate a company, recruiter, phone number or website.

Our guiding principle

Pause. Check. Proceed.

AI guidance + independent verification = better-informed decisions.
Important limitation

A low risk score is not a guarantee of safety, and a high score is not proof of fraud. Use JobShield as one source of guidance, not as a final decision-maker.
