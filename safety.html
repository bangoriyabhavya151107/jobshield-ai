CAREER SAFETY GUIDE

Stay sharp. Stay
one step ahead.

Simple habits can help you avoid common job and internship scams.

01
₹

Never pay to get hired

Be cautious if someone asks for registration, interview, training, equipment or security fees to offer you a job.

02
✉

Check recruiter identity

Compare the email domain with the company's official website. Contact HR using details you find independently.

03
⌁

Protect your information

Never share OTPs, passwords, card PINs or banking credentials with a recruiter or unknown person.

04
⏱

Don't be rushed

Pressure to accept immediately, secrecy demands or threats can be warning signs. Take time to check.

05
◎

Find the real website

Type the company's web address yourself. Watch for misspellings, extra words and lookalike domains.

06
▣

Ask for written details

Review the role, salary, location and employment terms. Be careful with vague offers and no proper interview.

IF SOMETHING FEELS WRONG

Pause before you respond.

Do not send money or sensitive information. Save messages and report suspected fraud through the appropriate official channels in your region.
