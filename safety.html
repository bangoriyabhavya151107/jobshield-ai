RED FLAGS TO NOTICE

Common job-scam patterns

One signal alone may not prove a scam. Look at the full context and verify independently.

₹
01 · PAYMENT

Upfront registration fees

Be cautious if someone asks you to pay for an interview, offer letter, training kit or guaranteed placement.

⚡
02 · PRESSURE

Unusual urgency

Pressure to accept immediately or skip normal interview steps deserves careful review.

✉
03 · EMAIL

Lookalike email domains

Check spelling carefully and compare the sender domain with contact details found independently.

🔐
04 · PRIVACY

Requests for OTPs or passwords

Never share passwords, one-time codes, banking PINs or remote-access permissions with a recruiter.

📄
05 · ROLE DETAILS

Vague job descriptions

Ask for a written role, responsibilities, reporting manager, salary structure and official hiring process.

🌐
06 · WEBSITE

Unverified websites

Type the official company domain yourself. Do not rely only on links sent in unsolicited messages.
